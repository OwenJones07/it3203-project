What Is Information Technology?

Information Technology can be understood as a field focused on selecting, implementing, maintaining, and supporting technology so that people and organizations can accomplish their goals. The Association for Computing Machinery describes IT as focusing on the design, implementation, and maintenance of technology solutions while supporting users and organizational needs.

What I find most interesting about IT is that it is not simply about understanding computers, but about understanding how technology can actually be used to make everyone's work easier, more efficient, secure, or reliable.

Areas IT Professionals Work In

Because of this variety, I see Information Technology as a field that connects many different aspects of computing and applies them to practical situations. IT professionals can work with:

  • Networks
  • Operating systems
  • Hardware
  • Software
  • Cybersecurity
  • Cloud services
  • Databases
  • Technical support

IT in Practice

Area Example task Who benefits
Networks Managing a network so an organization's systems stay connected Everyone who uses the network
Hardware and operating systems Configuring computers and troubleshooting hardware and software problems Employees and students using those computers
Databases Making sure a company's database is available, secure, and functioning properly The organization and its customers
Deployment and support Deploying an application and making sure users can access it Employees and customers

Why I Chose IT

I chose Information Technology because of my personal experience with it. Throughout the last two summers, I had the opportunity to intern with my previous high school's IT department. Before that experience, I was a Computer Game Design and Development major. After seeing the different parts of working at the school under the IT department, I felt a deep connection and passion for the field. I was able to learn how the school functions at a technological level, which was really interesting and informative.
