Abstract

Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Data Science, and Information Technology overlap in many ways, but each has its own focus. This site explains how Information Technology is the practical field of implementing, managing, and supporting technology for people and organizations. It compares IT with the other computing fields and shows why they work best together.

Key Terms

Information Technology
The field of selecting, implementing, maintaining, and supporting technology so people can reach their goals.
Infrastructure
The networks, servers, hardware, and software an organization depends on.
Deployment
Installing and setting up software or systems so users can use them.
Computing discipline
A recognized field of computing, such as CS, CE, SE, Data Science, or IT.
