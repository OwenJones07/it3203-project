Key Concepts

These are the main ideas behind how I understand Information Technology.

Information Technology

A field focused on selecting, implementing, maintaining, and supporting technology so people and organizations can accomplish their goals.

IT Infrastructure

The networks, operating systems, hardware, databases, and other systems an organization depends on, which IT professionals manage.

Technical Support

Helping the people who use technology solve problems and ensuring they can access the systems they need.

Deployment and Maintenance

Setting up applications and systems so users can access them, then keeping them working over time.

Cybersecurity

Keeping systems and data secure so that technology stays reliable. It is one of the areas IT professionals work in.

Levels of Abstraction

IT generally works at a higher level than hardware design, configuring and managing systems that computer engineers make possible.

Computing Ecosystem

The idea that CS, CE, SE, Data Science, and IT are different pieces that work together rather than separate, standalone fields.
